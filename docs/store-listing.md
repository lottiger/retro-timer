### Full description (indexed — keywords woven into natural copy)

Hourglass is a sand timer you flip with a tap — a calm, simple
alternative to ticking clocks and cluttered countdown apps.

Pick 30 seconds, 1, 1:30 or 2 minutes, or set a custom time on minute
and second wheels. The sand runs smoothly through the glass and the
amount of sand always matches the time remaining. Tap anywhere to flip
the hourglass and restart; hold to pause. When the last grain lands you
get a soft chime and a firm vibration — with a notification even if
your screen has locked.

CHESS CLOCK AND BOARD GAME TIMER
Switch to chess mode for two hourglasses, one per player. Tap anywhere
to end your turn and start your opponent's clock. Play 3+0 blitz or
10+5 and 15+10 rapid with real Fischer increment — banked time and all.
Or use turn-timer mode for board games and party games, where every
player gets a fresh 30 seconds to 3 minutes per turn.

A visual timer like this is perfect for studying and focus sessions,
homework, kids' screen time, workouts, meditation, tea and cooking —
anywhere an egg timer would do, but calmer.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No ads, no account, no tracking — nothing is collected
• Dark and light themes, following your system or your choice
• Keeps the screen awake while the sand runs
• Sound and haptics you can toggle
• Screen reader support
